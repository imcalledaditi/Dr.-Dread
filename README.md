# Dr. Dread

Welcome to **Dr. Dread** – Your brutally honest guide to navigating life's chaos! ⚡ Whether you're spiraling through an existential crisis or just looking for raw, no-nonsense advice, Dr. Dread is here to provide emotional clarity and brutally honest insights that will leave you questioning everything and laughing through it all.

## 📌 About
**Dr. Dread** isn’t your average wellness platform — it's an experience. A blend of dark humor, emotional wisdom, and unfiltered truth that confronts life's absurdities head-on. Whether you're struggling with overthinking, trapped in the cycle of doomscrolling, or simply trying to make sense of the madness, Dr. Dread is the companion you never knew you needed. No sugarcoating, no fluff — just fierce honesty and the kind of insight that might just change your life. 💀🖤

## 🔥 Features

### 🧠 Emotional Support
Get no-nonsense emotional guidance that cuts through the noise. Dr. Dread doesn’t just listen; it responds with sharp insight and raw truth, helping you confront your chaos without pitying you.

### 🎭 Dark Humor
Life’s absurd, and Dr. Dread knows it. Embrace the madness with humor that doesn’t shy away from the darker corners, making it easier to laugh through the existential crisis.

### 🔍 Existential Clarity
Dive deep into the chaos of your mind with a Muse that doesn’t shy away from tough truths. Whether you’re stuck in your head or facing the mess of the world, Dr. Dread helps you find clarity — and maybe even a reason to keep going.

### 🔒 Anonymous Conversations
Chat without leaving a trace. No sign-ups, no saved history, and no digital trail. Just you, your thoughts, and Dr. Dread, who doesn’t need your email to understand your existential mess.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** React.js with TypeScript (TSX) ⚛️
- **Chatbot:** Powered by Gemini API 🤖
- **Deployment:** Hosted on Vercel 🌍

## 🚀 How to Run Locally
Want to contribute or explore the project? Here’s how to run Dr. Dread locally:

1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/yourusername/Dr-Dread.git
2. Navigate to the project directory:
    ```bash
    cd Dr-Dread
3. Install dependencies:
    ```bash
    npm install
4. Start the development server:
    ```bash
    npm run dev
